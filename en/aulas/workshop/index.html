Lesson 8 · 20 min

Workshop and B2B community: from free to monthly advisory

The workshop is the cheapest way in and the one that generates the most work afterward: the team learns, the manager sees you in action, and the next conversation is the diagnosis. This lesson shows the 1-hour workshop in 5 parts, the small community of companies, and your plan with dates.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1Structure the workshop like a flight, in 5 parts
  2. 2Read the audience and prepare extra content
  3. 3Build the small community of companies
  4. 4Generate your plan with dates

Goal: design a 1-hour workshop in 5 parts and the ladder from free to monthly advisory, with a dated plan.

01 · The workshop is a flight

Parts 1, 2 and 3 fit in the first five minutes.

Click each block in the diagram to see what it does.

Teaching is a skill of repetition and feedback, not talent. The structure stands in for talent until it comes.

02 · Before and during the workshop

Four decisions that change the room.

1. The audience

An analyst wants the detail; the executive team wants the design and the return. Ask the client beforehand who will be in the room.

Required room: open with demos. Voluntary room: they want the fundamentals. Name the fear about jobs in the first few minutes.

2. Extra content

If one hour fits 30 slides, prepare 50: most of them are extra examples of the same idea.

Decide beforehand what to skip if the room talks a lot, and keep two backup blocks.

3. The silence

Ask the question and wait. Whoever gives in after three seconds teaches the room not to answer.

If nobody answers, make a light joke and move on. Don't push: your energy doesn't depend on the audience.

PhraseI can stay quiet for the next few minutes, or you can give me a hint so I can adapt the examples to your work.

4. Industry examples

Whoever uses examples from one industry attracts clients from that industry. Use the real vocabulary: enrollment, policy, appraisal report, deed.

If the clients who show up are from the wrong industry, look at your examples.

03 · The small community and the access ladder

Seven to ten companies, not seven hundred people. Ranges are orders of magnitude: adapt to your market.

1. Free tier

Basic templates, a monthly bulletin with what changed, questions answered in public, and the open office hours.

It needs to be good enough for someone to recommend it without ever having paid.

2. Paid tier

Advanced guides, real cases, long sessions with live building, members-only office hours.

A group of companies usually lands in the US$ 100 to 500 per company per month range. With 7 to 10, you know each one's problem.

3. Monthly advisory

A defined response window, with rules, not unlimited access to your life.

Whoever moves up has already proven, on the previous step, that they extract value.

4. Invitations

After the call: "instead of filling your inbox, I have a space where I answer questions like the ones we discussed".

After the workshop: "if you want to bring your own company's case, this is the space".

Destination (1 sentence): after this hour, you will be able to <do what>.

00-05 OPENING
- Hook: <image or demo running>
- So what: <what changes for those who pay attention>
- Path and flight plan: <warn about the technical stretch>
- Name the fear: <sentence>

05-20 MODULE 1 · foundation (light, lots of examples)
- Concept → when to use → before and after → example from the industry
- Question for the room (wait 15 s): <question>

20-40 MODULE 2 · the densest (at peak attention)
- <technical content warned about in the flight plan>
- Question for the room: <question>

40-55 MODULE 3 · applied (attention has already dropped)
- Day-to-day example: <what changes tomorrow morning>
- Vision example: <what changes in 12 months>

55-60 LANDING
- Recap 3 points · how to apply on Monday · what comes next
- Questions · invitation: <community or office hours>

BACKUP (if the room goes quiet): <block A>, <block B>
SKIP (if the room talks a lot): <block>
